MCB COMPARISONS WITH ETS BASE FORECASTS: RESULTS REPORTED IN THE SUBMITTED THESIS

The submitted thesis reports a separate Friedman/Nemenyi analysis comparing
ETS base forecasts with matching IComb and FLAIC specifications under 95%
Nemenyi comparisons. Each panel contains Base, IComb, and eight FLAIC variants.

At forecast horizons 1 and 12, the included FLAIC specifications were reported
to have significantly better average ranks than the ETS base forecasts.

At horizon 6, two FLAIC-S specifications were reported as NOT significantly
better than the ETS base forecasts:
  - Ridge (FLAIC-S), centred-unscaled PCA, p = 77.
  - Ridge (FLAIC-S), uncentred-unscaled PCA, p = 77.

This is a record of what the thesis reports, not a numerical recomputation in
this repository release. Run 09_mcb_with_base.R after the complete evaluation
objects are available to produce unrounded ranks, critical distances, and CSVs
for every method, preprocessing specification and horizon.

"Not significantly better" does not imply "significantly worse". Hierarchy
series are related through aggregation, so rank-based statistical comparisons
need cautious interpretation. The numerical outputs should be checked against
the statements above before publishing an empirical significance claim.
