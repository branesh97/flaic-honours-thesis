SUPPLEMENTARY MCB COMPARISONS WITH ETS BASE FORECASTS
====================================================

This short account reproduces the qualitative conclusion reported in the submitted
thesis (the footnote accompanying the MCB analysis). It is NOT a recomputation
from model output files. To produce the full numerical evidence and verify
these statements, run 09_mcb_with_base.R after 05_evaluate.R.

Method:
- The Friedman test and post-hoc Nemenyi critical-distance comparisons are
  conducted using each of the 112 hierarchy series as a ranking block.
- Forecast horizons h = 1, 6, 12 are considered.
- For each PCA centring/scaling specification, ridge and lasso are analysed
  separately. Each comparison includes the ETS Base, the matching IComb
  estimator, and the eight FLAIC augmentation dimensions.
- Adding Base changes the number of methods in each panel and therefore
  requires a new Nemenyi critical distance.

Reported findings:
- At h = 1 and h = 12, both IComb variants and all FLAIC variants have
  significantly better average ranks than the ETS base forecasts.
- At h = 6, this also holds except for TWO ridge-regularised FLAIC-S
  configurations, both using p = 77 principal components:
    * FLAIC-S; centred, unscaled PCA; p = 77; h = 6.
    * FLAIC-S; uncentred, unscaled PCA; p = 77; h = 6.
- These are exceptions to being significantly better than Base; they do not
  establish that the two FLAIC methods are significantly WORSE than Base.

Statistical caution:
The 112 series are linked through aggregation, so the ranking blocks are not
independent. Interpretation of test significance should be cautious.
